Metric feel guide

0°C = freezing
10°C = cool
20°C = comfortable
25°C = warm
30°C+ = hot
16°C dew point = getting humid
21°C+ dew point = very humid
25 mm+ rain = heavy daily rain

Korea weather intuition

  • Celsius is the everyday temperature frame.
  • Rain is usually discussed in millimeters.
  • Wind may appear as km/h or m/s, so both are shown.
  • Pressure is commonly shown in hPa.
